2. Data which cannot be linked to your person, for example through anonymisation, is not personal data. Processing (e.g. collection, storage, readout, retrieval, use, transmission, deletion or destruction) in accordance with Art. 4 No. 2 of the DS-GVO always requires a legal basis or your consent. 2. Right to correction or deletion
3. Right to limit processing
4. Right to object to the processing
5. Right to data transferability
6. Right to withdraw your consent
7. Right of appeal to a supervisory authority
8. Right to object to the processing
9.  Art. 21 DS-GVO allows you to object at any time, for reasons arising from your particular situation, to the processing of personal data relating to you that is carried out on the basis of Art. 6 Paragraph 1 S. 1 e or f DS-GVO.

You can revoke your consent to the processing of your personal data at any time with effect for the future.

1. **The nature and extent of data processing**. We collect personal data about you in the pre-contractual area and upon conclusion of the contract. This concerns for example e-mail address, password, full name of the licensee, location data of the licensee. In addition, depending on the licensee's voluntary choice, the following data is collected: profile picture, bio/description, telephone number, events created by the licensee, the title of the event, the description of the event, the date and time of the event, pictures in connection with the event Events in which the licensee has participated in the past, upcoming events in which the licensee will participate, announcement messages created by the licensee for the purpose of his event as well as ratings/feedback for an event organizer in which a licensee who has previously participated in an event participates

2. **Purpose of the data processing**. We collect and process this data exclusively for the purpose of contract execution or for the fulfilment of pre-contractual obligations.

3. **The legal basis** for this is Art. 6 para. 1 sentence 1 lit b) DS-GVO. If you have given your consent (voluntary information), the additional legal basis is Art. 6 para. 1 sentence 1 lit. a) DS-GVO.

4. **Storage period**. The data are deleted as soon as they are no longer required for the purpose of their processing. There may also be statutory storage obligations, for example commercial or tax storage obligations under the German Commercial Code (HGB) or the German Fiscal Code (AO). Insofar as such obligations to retain data exist, we will block or delete your data at the end of these retention obligations.

1. We only pass on your personal data to third parties if you have given your express consent to do so in accordance with Art. 6 para. 1 sentence 1 lit. a) DS-GVO. This is legally permissible and, in accordance with Art. 6 para. 1 sentence 1 lit. b) DS-GVO, is necessary for the fulfilment of a contractual relationship with you or the implementation of pre-contractual measures.

    According to Art. 6 (1) lit. c) DS-GVO, there is a legal obligation for the disclosure:

    We are legally obliged to transfer data to state authorities, e.g. tax authorities, social security institutions, health insurance companies, supervisory authorities and law enforcement agencies.

    The transfer of data pursuant to Art. 6 Paragraph 1 letter f) DS-GVO is necessary to safeguard legitimate company interests and to assert, exercise or defend legal claims, and there is no reason to assume that you have an overriding interest worthy of protection in not disclosing your data.

    In accordance with Art. 28 DS-GVO, we use external service providers, so-called contract processors, who are obliged to handle your data with care. In particular, we use such service providers in the following areas: IT, logistics, telecommunications.
